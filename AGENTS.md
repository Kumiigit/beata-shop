# T-shirt catalogue rules

- Offer 100% cotton in white, black and blue, and 100% polyester in white, black, blue and yellow.
- Restrict each design to its supplied colours. A black-only or white-only design must offer both cotton and polyester in that colour.
- Name each option with its fabric and colour, using the existing format `100% Cotton Black` / `100% Polyester Black`. Match the original colour photo and any back photo for both fabrics.
- Established prices are £6.99 for polyester and £7.99 for cotton. Preserve existing sizes, stock, collection and visibility unless requested otherwise.
- Descriptions must reflect the actual fabric options; do not describe every T-shirt as polyester-only or cotton-only.
